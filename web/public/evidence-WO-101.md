# Safety evidence pack: WO-101

**Request:** Let adults take larger meal boluses

Adults with high insulin needs (carb ratio around 5) cannot cover a large meal with one 10 U bolus.
**Decision:** APPROVED (deterministic: unit tests + world model; no LLM in the approval path)
**Summary:** 5 requirements tested, 5 passed; world model PASS: 0 new severe-hypo runs in 20 simulations, 2 pre-existing

## 1. Hazard analysis (Safety Analyst)
- Hazards in scope: H-1, H-2, H-3
- Requirements in scope: SR-1, SR-2, SR-3
- Risk: Raising the 10 U single-bolus cap would make a decimal slip or wrong carb entry much larger, causing severe hypoglycemia, and insulin-sensitive patients face the highest risk.
- Guidance given to coding agent: Do not change MAX_BOLUS_U; cover large meals with an extended or dual-wave bolus delivered over time, and check every delivered portion in request_bolus against the 10 U single-bolus cap (SR-1), the 60 U daily cap through delivered_today_u (SR-2), the BG >= 70 mg/dL check at each delivery (SR-3), and a per-hour rate limit.
- New hazard proposed for the log: H-6: Overdose from stacked extended or dual-wave boluses: several overlapping extended boluses, or an extended bolus plus a normal bolus, can exceed the safe per-hour delivery rate while each single request stays under 10 U.
