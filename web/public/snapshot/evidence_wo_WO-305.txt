# Safety evidence pack: WO-305

**Request:** Raise the single-bolus limit to 25 U

Adults with high insulin needs cannot cover a large meal with one 10 U bolus. This is a one-line config change: set MAX_BOLUS_U in demo/pump/pump.h to 25.0.
**Decision:** APPROVED (deterministic: unit tests + world model; no LLM in the approval path)
**Summary:** 5 requirements tested, 5 passed; world model PASS: 0 new severe-hypo runs in 20 simulations, 2 pre-existing

## 1. Hazard analysis (Safety Analyst)
- Hazards in scope: H-1, H-2, H-3
- Requirements in scope: SR-1, SR-2, SR-3
- Risk: Raising MAX_BOLUS_U to 25.0 breaks SR-1 (single bolus never exceeds 10 U), so the pump would accept a 25 U bolus; a decimal slip (for example 2.5 typed as 25) would deliver 10x the intended dose and cause severe hypoglycemia, and one such bolus would use 42% of the 60 U daily cap.
- Guidance given to coding agent: Do not change MAX_BOLUS_U from 10.0, because SR-1 and the Oct 28 clinical safety review forbid raising the cap; cover large meals with an extended or dual-wave bolus in which each delivered portion stays at or below 10 U, per-hour delivery stays within the max bolus rate, and SR-2 (60 U daily cap) and SR-3 (no bolus below 70 mg/dL) still apply; escalate to clinical review for a formal SR-1 change if the 25 U request stands.
- New hazard proposed for the log: Bigger damage from input errors: at a 25 U cap a decimal slip or extra digit (2.5 typed as 25) is no longer rejected, and the pump has no confirmation step or sanity check against the user's usual dose for large boluses.
