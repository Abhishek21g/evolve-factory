# Safety evidence pack: WO-100

**Request:** Let adults take larger meal boluses
**Decision:** APPROVED (deterministic: unit tests + world model; no LLM in the approval path)
**Summary:** 5 requirements tested, 5 passed; world model PASS: 0 new severe-hypo runs in 20 simulations, 2 pre-existing

## 1. Hazard analysis (Safety Analyst)
- Hazards in scope: H-1, H-2, H-3
- Requirements in scope: SR-1, SR-2, SR-3
- Risk: Raising the size of a meal bolus can break the 10 U single-bolus cap (SR-1) or use up the 60 U daily cap early, which can cause severe hypoglycemia, especially when BG is already low or near 70 mg/dL.
- Guidance given to coding agent: Do not change MAX_BOLUS_U, MAX_DAILY_U or MIN_BG_FOR_BOLUS; deliver a large meal dose through the existing extended-bolus interface as parts of 10 U or less, add a per-hour delivery limit, and before each part check the BG gate, the daily total (delivered_today_u) and the hourly limit so that SR-1, SR-2 and SR-3 still pass.
- New hazard proposed for the log: Bolus stacking: back-to-back or extended bolus parts inside one hour can go over the maximum hourly delivery rate, and the log has no per-hour limit for this; a related risk is that a non-adult profile gets adult-sized meal doses.
