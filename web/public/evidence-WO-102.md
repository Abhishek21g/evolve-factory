## 1. Hazard analysis (Safety Analyst)
- Hazards in scope: H-1, H-2, H-4, H-5
- Requirements in scope: SR-1, SR-2, SR-4, SR-5
- Risk: If the pump does not raise the interruption alarm, the patient misses insulin and may become hyperglycemic. If the pump or the user then resumes or re-programs the bolus without counting the units already delivered, the patient may get a stacked overdose.
- Guidance given to coding agent: Raise the alarm only for interruption causes that the pump has detected (occlusion via check_occlusion, watchdog via check_watchdog, user stop, or power loss), and report both delivered and undelivered units. Do not auto-resume or auto-deliver the remainder. Route any re-delivery through request_bolus so the MAX_BOLUS_U, MAX_DAILY_U and MIN_BG_FOR_BOLUS checks still apply, leave every existing constant and timing value unchanged, and add a traceable test for the alarm next to the SR-1, SR-2, SR-4 and SR-5 re-verification.
- New hazard proposed for the log: H-6: Insulin stacking or missed delivery after an extended bolus is interrupted, because the remainder is resumed or re-programmed without counting units already delivered, or because the interruption is not announced (for example after a power loss or reset).
