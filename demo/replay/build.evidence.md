# Safety evidence pack: WO-200

**Request:** Raise the single-bolus limit to 25 U

Adults with high insulin needs cannot cover a large meal with one 10 U bolus. This is a one-line config change: set MAX_BOLUS_U in demo/pump/pump.h to 25.0.
**Decision:** APPROVED (deterministic: unit tests + world model; no LLM in the approval path)
**Summary:** 5 requirements tested, 5 passed; world model PASS: 0 new severe-hypo runs in 20 simulations, 2 pre-existing

## 1. Hazard analysis (Safety Analyst)
- Hazards in scope: H-1, H-2, H-3
- Requirements in scope: SR-1, SR-2, SR-3
- Risk: Raising MAX_BOLUS_U to 25 directly breaks SR-1. A decimal slip such as 2.5 U entered as 25 U would then deliver in one bolus, and two such boluses would use 50 of the 60 U daily cap, which could cause severe hypoglycemia or death.
- Guidance given to coding agent: Reject the one-line change and keep MAX_BOLUS_U at 10.0 because the Oct 28 clinical safety review prohibits raising it. Meet the large-meal need with an extended or dual-wave bolus in which each delivered portion is 10 U or less, SR-2 (60 U daily cap) and SR-3 (no bolus below 70 mg/dL) still apply in request_bolus, the hourly rate is limited, and a confirmation step guards large entries. Update the ISO 14971 hazard log, keep traceability to test_safety, and add UVA/Padova simulation evidence.
- New hazard proposed for the log: Use-error overdose from a decimal or keypad slip (for example, 2.5 U entered as 25 U) that a higher single-bolus cap would accept without any check against the patient's own dosing profile.
